About the Poet

Faiz Ahmed Faiz was one of the most celebrated Urdu poets of the twentieth century. Born in Sialkot in 1911, he was known for combining themes of love, social justice, and revolution in his poetry. His literary works continue to inspire readers across generations.

Famous Literary Works

Some of his famous works include:

  1. Naqsh-e-Faryadi
  2. Dast-e-Saba
  3. Zindan Nama
  4. Dast-e-Tah-e-Sang

Other Contributions

  • Urdu poetry
  • Social and political writing
  • Literary essays

My Favourite Poem

Mujh Se Pehli Si Mohabbat

My favourite poem by Faiz Ahmed Faiz is "Mujh Se Pehli Si Mohabbat". This beautiful nazm explores love, human suffering, and social realities. I admire how Faiz connects personal emotions with the problems of society.
